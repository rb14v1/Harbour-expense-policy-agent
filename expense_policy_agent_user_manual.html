A. Introduction

What Is the Expense Policy Agent?

The Expense Policy Agent is a cloud-based application that automates the receipt processing and expense management workflow. It helps employees submit receipts for business expenses and enables finance teams to review, approve, or reject submissions according to company expense policies.

Why Use This Application?

  • Speed: Automatic receipt scanning eliminates manual data entry.
  • Accuracy: Azure Document Intelligence extracts receipt details reliably.
  • Duplicate Prevention: Prevents the same receipt from being submitted twice.
  • Policy Compliance: Validates receipts against company expense policies automatically.
  • Transparency: Real-time status updates keep employees informed.
  • Efficiency: Finance team gets organized submissions ready for approval.

Who Uses This Application?

Employees use this application to:

  • Upload receipt images or PDF files
  • Verify extracted receipt information
  • Select expense categories when needed
  • Track receipt processing status
  • View approval or rejection decisions

Finance Team Members use this application to:

  • Review submitted receipts in Microsoft Teams
  • Verify receipt details and policy compliance
  • Approve or reject expense submissions
  • Provide feedback on policy violations
  • Maintain audit trail of all decisions
Key Takeaway: This application automates routine receipt processing so both employees and finance team can focus on resolving edge cases and special approvals.

B. Employee Receipt Upload Workflow

Complete Step-by-Step Process

Step 1: Access the Application

Open your web browser and navigate to the Expense Policy Agent login page. Sign in with your company credentials.

Step 2: Navigate to Receipt Upload

On the main dashboard, select the "Upload Receipt" option to access the receipt upload form.

Step 3: Select and Upload Receipt File

Click the upload button and select your receipt file. Supported formats are:

  • PDF (.pdf) - Recommended for email receipts
  • JPEG (.jpg, .jpeg) - Photos taken with your phone
  • PNG (.png) - Screenshots of digital receipts

File size limit: Maximum 10 MB per receipt.

Step 4: Upload Accepted and Processing Begins

The system confirms your file was accepted and displays "Processing..." status. The system now:

  • Stores your original receipt file securely in cloud storage
  • Calculates a unique fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of your file
  • Checks if this exact receipt was already submitted (exact duplicate detection)
Step 5A (If Exact Duplicate): Rejected Immediately

If the system recognizes your file as identical to a previously uploaded receipt:

  • Status shows: Exact Duplicate
  • Message: "This receipt has already been uploaded."
  • No further processing occurs
  • You must upload a different receipt or contact Finance if this is an error
Step 5B (If Unique): Continue to OCR

If no identical file exists, the system proceeds with automatic information extraction:

  • Status shows: OCR Processing
  • Azure Document Intelligence analyzes your receipt image
  • The system extracts key fields:
  • Merchant/vendor name (store or restaurant where you purchased)
  • Transaction date (when the purchase was made)
  • Total amount (total cost)
  • Tax amount (if shown on receipt)
  • Currency (currency of the transaction)
  • Payment method (cash, card, etc.)
  • Line items (individual purchases, if detailed on receipt)
Step 6: Automatic Category Classification

The system analyzes the extracted receipt information to automatically determine the expense category:

  • The system looks for keywords in the merchant name (e.g., "taxi", "restaurant", "hotel")
  • It uses advanced text analysis to understand the receipt context
  • A confidence score (0-100%) is calculated

Two possible outcomes:

  • High Confidence (≥75%): Category is automatically assigned. For example, a receipt from "Starbucks" is automatically classified as "Food: Breakfast or Lunch".
  • Low Confidence (<75%): System prompts you to manually select from a list of category choices. This ensures accuracy when the category is unclear.
Step 7 (If Manual Selection Needed): Choose Category

If confidence is low, a form appears showing available expense categories. Review the choices and select the most appropriate one for your receipt. Categories include (examples):

  • Accommodation/Hotel
  • Transport: Flights, Taxis/Cab/Uber, Car Rental, etc.
  • Food: Breakfast/Lunch, Dinner, Per Diem
  • Software, Training, Client Entertainment
  • Mileage (various options by region and vehicle type)
  • Other
Step 8: Semantic Duplicate Detection

After category assignment, the system checks whether this expense is a duplicate of a previous submission:

  • Compares merchant name, date, and amount with previous receipts
  • Looks for matching invoice numbers (strong indicator)
  • Calculates a similarity score

If potential duplicate found:

  • Status shows: Semantic Duplicate
  • Message explains why it appears to be a duplicate
  • Processing stops; you must clarify with Finance

If unique: Processing continues to policy validation

Step 9: Policy Validation

The system automatically checks your receipt against company expense policies:

  • Receipt Age Policy: Receipt must not be older than 35 days
  • Category Policies: Different categories have different rules (e.g., meal limits, mileage rates)

Two possible outcomes:

  • Passes: Status shows Needs Review - receipt is forwarded to Finance team
  • Fails: Status shows Rejected - Processing stops with reason
Step 10: Finance Review (if Policy Passed)

Receipts that passed all checks are sent to the Finance team for review:

  • Status shows: Needs Review
  • Finance team verifies the receipt and extracted information
  • Finance can approve the expense or reject it with a reason
Step 11: Final Status and Notification

Once Finance completes their review, your receipt status updates:

  • Approved: Approved - Your expense is approved for reimbursement
  • Rejected: Rejected - Finance has not approved your expense; a reason is provided

The updated status appears in your receipt history immediately.

Step 12: View Receipt History

At any time, you can view all your submitted receipts and their current status. The history shows:

  • Receipt filename and date uploaded
  • Merchant name, transaction date, and amount
  • Current status (processing, approved, rejected, etc.)
  • Category assigned
  • Any messages from the system or Finance team

Understanding Status Transitions

Automatic Processing Statuses

The following statuses occur automatically during system processing and require no action from you:

  • UPLOADED → HASHING → QUEUED_FOR_OCR → OCR_PROCESSING → EXTRACTED → NORMALIZED → DUPLICATE_CHECKING → POLICY_PROCESSING

These transitions typically complete within seconds to minutes.

Final Decision Statuses

Once a receipt reaches any of these statuses, processing stops and no further changes occur unless you contact Finance:

  • APPROVED (Success - your expense is approved)
  • REJECTED (Final - policy violation or Finance rejection)
  • EXACT_DUPLICATE (Final - re-upload not needed)
  • SEMANTIC_DUPLICATE (Pending - clarification with Finance may resolve)
  • FAILED (Error - requires investigation)

NEEDS_REVIEW Status

Important: This is NOT a final status. It means Finance is reviewing your receipt. The status will eventually change to APPROVED or REJECTED based on Finance's decision.

Status Check Tips:
  • Refresh your browser to see the latest status.
  • Most receipts progress through all statuses automatically within 5-10 minutes.
  • If a receipt is stuck on NEEDS_REVIEW for more than one business day, contact Finance.
  • The application displays the reason if a receipt is rejected; read it carefully to understand what went wrong.

D. Finance Workflow in Microsoft Teams

Overview for Finance Team Members

Finance team members interact with the Expense Policy Agent through Microsoft Teams. The integration provides a dedicated Finance Dashboard where you can review all pending receipts, view details, and make approval or rejection decisions without leaving Teams.

Accessing the Finance Dashboard

  1. Open Microsoft Teams
  2. Navigate to the Expense Policy Agent team or channel
  3. Select the "Finance Review" tab (if configured)
  4. The Finance Dashboard loads, showing all pending receipts for review

Finance Dashboard Features

Receipt List View

The main dashboard displays a list of all receipts with the following information:

  • Original Filename: The receipt document filename uploaded by the employee
  • Merchant/Vendor: Where the expense occurred (restaurant, hotel, airline, etc.)
  • Transaction Date: When the purchase was made
  • Amount: Total expense amount
  • Category: Expense category assigned (automatically or manually)
  • Status: Current processing status (should be NEEDS_REVIEW for items in the dashboard)
  • Employee: Who submitted the receipt

Filtering and Searching

Use filters to narrow down which receipts to review:

  • By Status: Filter to show only NEEDS_REVIEW or other statuses
  • By Category: Show only a specific expense category
  • By Vendor: Search by merchant name
  • By Date Range: Show receipts from a specific time period
  • By Amount Range: Filter high-value or low-value expenses

Reviewing a Receipt

Step 1: Select a Receipt from the List

Click on a receipt in the dashboard to open the review details. An Adaptive Card appears showing:

  • Receipt filename
  • Extracted merchant and transaction details
  • Calculated amount and currency
  • Assigned category and confidence
  • All extracted line items (if detailed receipt)
  • Employee name who submitted

Step 2: Preview Receipt Image/PDF

Click the "Preview" or "View Receipt" button to open the original receipt file in your browser. This allows you to visually verify that:

  • The OCR extraction is accurate
  • The merchant and amount are correctly identified
  • The receipt is legitimate and readable
  • No signs of fraud or alteration

Step 3: Verify Policy Compliance

Review whether the receipt complies with company policy:

  • Is the receipt within 35 days of the transaction date?
  • Does the category have amount limits? Is the expense under the limit?
  • Are there any special rules for this category in your region?
  • Is the employee eligible for reimbursement in this category?

Step 4: Make a Decision

After reviewing, select one of the action buttons in the Adaptive Card:

Approve Button

  • Click to approve the expense for reimbursement
  • Receipt status changes to APPROVED
  • Employee is notified of approval
  • Expense proceeds to reimbursement processing per company procedure

Reject Button

  • Click to reject the expense
  • A dialog may appear asking for a rejection reason
  • Enter a brief explanation (e.g., "Exceeds meal limit", "Too old", "Personal expense")
  • Receipt status changes to REJECTED
  • Employee sees the rejection and reason in their receipt history
Important: Once you approve or reject a receipt, the decision is recorded in the audit trail. You cannot undo a decision, so verify the receipt carefully before clicking either button.

Common Finance Scenarios

Scenario: Expense Exceeds Category Limit

You see a lunch receipt for $50, but the category limit for "Food: Lunch" is $15.

  • Policy status: The system should have already rejected this (status: REJECTED)
  • If it reached you: The system incorrectly calculated or the receipt is mislabeled
  • Action: Reject with reason "Exceeds meal category limit"

Scenario: Receipt Seems Fraudulent

A receipt appears altered or suspicious (smudged amounts, inconsistent fonts).

  • Action: Reject with reason "Receipt appears altered or suspicious; requires employee clarification"
  • Follow-up: Contact the employee if your company protocol requires

Scenario: Receipt Is Legitimate but Policy-Exempt

A receipt might violate policy but has special approval (e.g., client entertainment, exception approval).

  • Action: If authorized, approve with internal note
  • Coordination: Ensure special approval is documented separately if required

E. Common Scenarios and Troubleshooting

Scenario 1: Receipt Uploads Successfully

What you see: "Processing..." status, then status changes through automatic checks.

What's happening: System checks for exact duplicate, performs OCR, classifies category, checks for semantic duplicate, validates policy.

Next step: Monitor status. If final status is APPROVED or REJECTED, check your receipt history.

Scenario 2: System Cannot Determine Category

What you see: Prompt asking you to select expense category manually.

Why: The system's confidence in auto-classification was below threshold (< 75%).

What to do: Select the most appropriate category from the list provided. If unsure, ask your manager or Finance team.

Scenario 3: Receipt Information Missing

What you see: Status FAILED with message "Document does not appear to be a valid receipt."

Why: OCR extraction failed or extracted incomplete data (missing merchant, total, or required fields).

What to do: Upload a different receipt or a clearer image/PDF of the same receipt. Ensure:

  • Receipt is not too blurry or faded
  • Merchant name is clearly visible
  • Total amount is legible
  • Date is readable

Scenario 4: Duplicate Detected

What you see: Status EXACT_DUPLICATE or SEMANTIC_DUPLICATE with message indicating duplicate found.

Why: File is identical to a previous upload, or receipt details match a previous submission closely.

What to do:

  • Exact Duplicate: Do not re-upload the same file. Contact Finance if you believe it's an error.
  • Semantic Duplicate: Contact Finance to explain if this is a legitimate second submission (e.g., different trip leg, duplicate receipt issued).

Scenario 5: Policy Validation Fails

What you see: Status REJECTED with message like "Receipt is older than 35 days" or "Exceeds meal limit."

Why: Receipt violates company expense policy.

What to do: Read the rejection reason carefully.

  • If policy is strict, contact your manager or Finance for possible exception.
  • If mislabeled, try uploading again with correct category.
  • If receipt date was entered wrong, contact Finance to update.

Scenario 6: Expense Awaits Finance Review

What you see: Status NEEDS_REVIEW for more than a few minutes.

Why: Receipt passed all automatic checks and is in the Finance team's review queue.

What to do:

  • Wait for Finance to review (typically 1-3 business days)
  • Status will update to APPROVED or REJECTED
  • If waiting longer than expected, contact Finance directly

Scenario 7: Finance Approves Expense

What you see: Status APPROVED with confirmation message.

What this means: Finance team has approved your expense for reimbursement.

Next step: Reimbursement will be processed according to your company's payment procedure (direct deposit, check, etc.).

Scenario 8: Finance Rejects Expense

What you see: Status REJECTED with reason provided (e.g., "Personal expense", "Incomplete receipt", "Exceeded limit").

What to do:

  • Read the rejection reason carefully
  • If you believe it's an error, contact Finance with clarification
  • If legitimate, you may absorb the expense or request manager approval

Scenario 9: Application Page Fails to Load

What you see: Error message or blank page when opening receipt upload or history.

What to do:

  • Refresh your browser (Ctrl+R or Cmd+R)
  • Clear browser cache if refresh doesn't help
  • Try a different browser or device
  • Check your internet connection
  • If still failing, contact IT Support with error message/screenshot

Scenario 10: Azure Storage Temporarily Unavailable

What you see: Error uploading receipt, or status stuck on "UPLOADING" for extended time.

Why: Brief cloud service outage or network connectivity issue.

What to do:

  • Wait a few minutes and try again
  • If problem persists, contact IT Support
  • Keep the receipt available for later upload

F. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I upload a receipt?

Log into the Expense Policy Agent, click "Upload Receipt", select a PDF or image file (JPG/PNG), and submit. The file must be under 10 MB. The system will confirm receipt and begin processing automatically.

Q: What file formats are supported?

PDF (.pdf), JPEG (.jpg, .jpeg), and PNG (.png) are supported. If your receipt is in another format (e.g., screenshot as a WebP), save or convert it to one of the supported formats first.

Q: How do I know whether my receipt was processed?

Open your receipt history to see all submitted receipts and their statuses. The status updates automatically as processing progresses. Most receipts complete all checks within 5-10 minutes.

Q: What does my expense status mean?

See Section C (Expense Statuses) for a complete table explaining each status, what caused it, and what you should do. Quickly: APPROVED = done and approved; REJECTED = not approved; NEEDS_REVIEW = waiting for Finance; others are intermediate processing states.

Q: Why might I need to select a category manually?

The system uses keyword and semantic analysis to auto-classify receipts. If confidence is low (< 75%), it asks you to select manually. This ensures accuracy. Choose the category that best describes your expense. When in doubt, ask your manager.

Q: What happens if a duplicate is detected?

Exact Duplicate: Your file is identical to one uploaded before. Do not re-upload the same file; contact Finance if it's an error. Semantic Duplicate: Your receipt details closely match a previous submission. Contact Finance if it's a legitimate separate expense (e.g., different trip).

Q: How can I tell whether Finance has reviewed my expense?

If your status is APPROVED or REJECTED, Finance has completed review. APPROVED means it's approved for reimbursement. REJECTED means Finance did not approve; read the reason for details. NEEDS_REVIEW means Finance is still reviewing.

Q: How does a Finance user approve or reject an expense in Teams?

Finance opens the Finance Dashboard in Teams, selects a pending receipt, views the details and receipt preview, and clicks either the Approve or Reject button. Approvals are immediate; rejections may include a brief reason.

Q: Can I upload multiple receipts at once?

Upload receipts one at a time through the application. Each receipt is processed independently. You can upload your next receipt while the previous one is processing.

Q: What if I uploaded the wrong file by mistake?

If processing has not completed, contact Finance immediately to prevent it from being reviewed. If processing is complete and receipt is rejected, simply do not resubmit it. If processing is complete and receipt is approved, contact Finance to discuss if cancellation is possible.

Q: How long does receipt processing take?

Automatic checks (extraction, duplicate detection, policy validation) typically complete in 5-10 minutes. Finance review (NEEDS_REVIEW status) typically takes 1-3 business days depending on volume.

Q: What should I do if receipt processing fails?

If status shows FAILED or OCR_FAILED, the receipt image was too blurry or unreadable. Try uploading a clearer image or PDF version. Ensure the receipt file is legible and in good condition.

Q: Where can I get help if something goes wrong?

Contact your Finance department or IT Support. Provide your receipt ID (shown in receipt history) and the status message so they can investigate.

Q: Is my receipt data secure?

Yes. Receipt files are stored in encrypted cloud storage (Azure Blob Storage). Extracted data is stored in a secure database. Access is restricted to authorized Finance team members. Data is not shared with external parties.

Q: Can I delete a receipt from history?

Receipts are retained permanently for audit and compliance purposes. You cannot delete a receipt. If you believe a receipt should not have been approved, contact Finance immediately.

Q: What happens if I reject a Finance approval decision?

You cannot reject a Finance decision. Once Finance approves or rejects, the decision is final and recorded in the audit trail. If you believe the decision is incorrect, contact Finance directly to discuss.

  • Approved receipts proceed to reimbursement automatically
  • Rejected receipts notify employee; they can appeal or clarify

Glossary

OCR (Optical Character Recognition)
Technology that reads images and extracts text and data. Used to automatically pull merchant name, amount, date, and other information from receipt images.
Azure Document Intelligence
Microsoft's cloud service that analyzes documents (receipts, invoices) and extracts structured data automatically. This is the OCR system used by Expense Policy Agent.
Azure Blob Storage
Microsoft's secure cloud file storage service. Original receipt files are stored here for archiving and audit purposes.
SHA-256 Hash / File Fingerprint
A unique identifier calculated from a file's content. Two files with identical content will have the same hash, even if named differently. Used to detect exact duplicates.
Semantic Duplicate
A receipt that may be a duplicate based on matching merchant, date, and amount—even though the file is different. Examples: rescanned receipt, screenshot vs. PDF, compressed image version.
Exact Duplicate
A receipt file that is bit-for-bit identical to a previously uploaded receipt. Same file, different upload attempt.
Policy Validation
Automatic check to ensure receipt complies with company expense rules (e.g., age limit, category limits). Receipts failing this check are auto-rejected.
Adaptive Card
A rich interactive message format used in Microsoft Teams. Displays receipt information and action buttons (Approve/Reject) in the Finance Dashboard.
Finance Dashboard
A Teams tab that shows Finance team members all pending receipts (NEEDS_REVIEW status) with options to approve or reject.
Expense Category
A classification of what type of business expense this is (e.g., Transport: Taxis, Food: Lunch, Accommodation). Used for policy validation and reporting.
Confidence Score
A percentage (0-100%) indicating how confident the system is in its automatic category classification. High confidence (≥ 75%) auto-assigns the category. Low confidence (< 75%) prompts manual selection.
Audit Trail
A permanent record of all actions taken on a receipt: who uploaded it, when, what decisions were made, who made them, and when. Cannot be modified or deleted.
